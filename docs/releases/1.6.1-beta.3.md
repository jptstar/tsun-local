# TSUN Local 1.6.1-beta.3

This beta corrects the daily-energy recovery behavior introduced in beta.2 while retaining all communication-resilience improvements from beta.1 and beta.2.

## Daily energy after an offline midnight rollover

- Every `*_energy_today` entity still resets locally to `0 kWh` at local midnight while the inverter/logger is sleeping or offline.
- When the inverter returns, the **first successful protocol poll containing a fresh daily-energy value is published immediately**.
- The extra two-successful-poll hold from beta.2 has been removed, so a valid value such as `3.4 kWh` no longer remains hidden behind the locally generated `0 kWh` for another polling interval.
- Total-energy restoration is unchanged.
- Instantaneous voltage/current/power measurements remain unavailable while the inverter is offline.

Example expected behavior: yesterday ends at `2.5 kWh`; at midnight the daily counter becomes `0 kWh` while the inverter remains offline; if the inverter reconnects at noon and reports `3.4 kWh`, Home Assistant shows `3.4 kWh` on that first valid update.

## Communication resilience retained

- 1097, 02B0 and 1511 retain persistent healthy-cycle TCP sessions and one bounded reconnect/retry after a failed block.
- Existing per-logger FIFO and adaptive polling behavior are unchanged.
- No validated 02B0 or 1511 register range is removed, merged or reinterpreted.
- Existing 1097 transient reconnect protection remains unchanged.

## Regression safety

- The production correction is limited to the shared Home Assistant sensor restoration layer.
- One legacy 1511 unit-test fixture was aligned with the persistent-session transport already released in beta.1; production 1511 transport code was not changed by this correction.
- Complete unit tests, HACS validation and Home Assistant Hassfest must pass before publication.

## What to test

1. Let the inverter remain offline across local midnight and verify daily AC/PV energy becomes `0 kWh`.
2. Reconnect or wait for sunrise and verify the first fresh daily-energy value appears immediately.
3. Verify total AC/PV energy retains its previous valid value while offline.
4. Verify instantaneous measurements remain unavailable while offline and recover normally afterward.
5. Watch communication stability on 1097, 02B0 and 1511 over several hours.

## Safety

TSUN Local remains local and read-only. This beta adds no inverter configuration or control writes.

TSUN Local at a glance

One clean integration for three local TSUN protocol families, with everyday entities visible and expert diagnostics kept out of the way.

🔌 1511 · 02B0 · 1097TITAN, GEN3 / GEN3 PLUS and experimental 1097 support.
🔍 Plug & play identificationNo protocol configuration in most cases. TSUN Local identifies a supported local protocol from the logger.
☀️ Dynamic PV detectionPV inputs appear progressively as the inverter exposes them.
🚨 Clear alarmsLocalized active alarm names without a wall of permanent entities.
🌍 Eight languagesEntities and active alarm wording follow Home Assistant.
🔒 Read-onlyNo inverter configuration or protection-setting writes.

1.5.1 beta: MP3000 field-validation update

The 1.5.0 presentation and alarm architecture stay intact. Beta 3 consolidates the MP3000 diagnostics, localized alarm-name entity and logger RSSI fixes.

📶 Wi-Fi RSSI fallback fixedA valid index page without RSSI no longer stops the search. TSUN Local continues until `/status.html` exposes `cover_sta_rssi`.
🛡️ 10 A1/21 diagnosticsTen additional MP3000 field-validation sensors are available as advanced entities and remain disabled by default.
🚨 Active alarm namesA dedicated sensor exposes localized active alarm text directly in Home Assistant. Internal Axxx identifiers stay diagnostic-only.
📐 Corrected coefficient0x07EF raw 4000 is decoded with ×0.01 as 40.00 %/Hz.

TSUN Local and Home Assistant FAQ

Quick answers for owners looking for a local TSUN microinverter integration.

Does TSUN Local require the cloud?No. Supported measurements are read directly over the local network. The integration does not require a cloud account or a proxy.
Which TSUN microinverters are validated?The TSOL-MP3000 is validated with protocol 1511 and the TSOL-MX500 with 02B0. Other models may work when they expose a supported protocol.
What is beta in 1.5.1-beta.3?The normal telemetry and 1.5.0 alarm interface remain intact; the new MP3000 semantic diagnostics are intentionally labelled as field-validation candidates.

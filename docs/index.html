TSUN Local at a glance

One clean integration for three local TSUN protocol families, with everyday entities visible and expert diagnostics kept out of the way.

🔌 1511 · 02B0 · 1097TITAN, GEN3 PLUS and experimental GEN3 support.
🔍 Automatic identificationThe supported local protocol is identified from the logger where available.
☀️ Dynamic PV detectionPV inputs appear progressively as the inverter exposes them.
🚨 Clear alarmsActive names and local codes without a wall of permanent entities.
🌍 Eight languagesEntities and active alarm wording follow Home Assistant.
🔒 Read-onlyNo inverter configuration or protection-setting writes.

Your TSUN inverter may already work

Useful entities first, advanced data on demand

Enabled by default

  • PV voltage, current, power and energy
  • AC voltage, current, frequency, power and energy
  • Inverter alarm and active alarm count
  • Communication and logger information
  • Online state and manual refresh

Advanced diagnostics

  • Grid-protection thresholds and timing
  • Inverter and ambient temperature where available
  • Power, protocol and insulation diagnostics
  • Complete raw alarm words, disabled by default
  • Read-only throughout

TSUN Local 1.4 — Home Assistant integration for TSUN micro-inverters

TSUN Local connects compatible TSUN solar micro-inverters directly to Home Assistant over the local network. Version 1.4 expands compatibility around three local protocol families: 1511, 02B0 and experimental 1097.

Compatibility candidates in 1.4

1511 · TITAN: validated on TSOL-MP3000; TSOL-MP2250 and TSOL-MS3000 are strong compatibility candidates.

02B0 · GEN3 PLUS: validated on TSOL-MX500. Likely candidates include TSOL-MX450, TSOL-MX800, TSOL-MX1000, TSOL-MX3000, TSOL-MS800, TSOL-MS1600, TSOL-MS1800 and TSOL-MS2000, with applicable -D variants.

1097 · GEN3: experimental candidates include TSOL-MS300, TSOL-MS350, TSOL-MS400, TSOL-MS600, TSOL-MS700, TSOL-MS800 and TSOL-MS3000.

Validated means confirmed on real hardware. Compatibility candidates still require broader real-device feedback.

Field-validation corrections in 1.4.0

Real-device validation on MP3000 / 1511 and MX500 / 02B0 refined several diagnostics in the republished stable 1.4.0 release.

  • Grid-protection timing values remain native seconds, with legacy beta-era automatic ms display choices migrated back to s.
  • On validated MP3000 hardware, raw alarm bit 0x2000 (8192) observed at dawn, dusk and very low irradiance remains visible but no longer reports a fault by itself; the operating state reports Standby — low solar input.
  • TITAN registers 3017 and 3028 remain unscaled raw decimal measurement sensors so their history can be charted while the temperature mapping is validated. No temperature offset is applied yet.
  • 02B0 register 0x202C is exposed as a raw output coefficient until its encoding is confirmed, rather than being labelled as a percentage.

TSUN Local preserves the raw diagnostic values and deliberately avoids claiming an unconfirmed vendor bit-name or temperature offset.

1097 remains experimental

The 1097 adapter is included in 1.4 for community validation. It can expose standard inverter/PV telemetry plus advanced diagnostics such as protocol version, inverter version, temperature, insulation RX/RY, country/profile raw value and designed power where available.

Install TSUN Local 1.4

Install TSUN Local through HACS, restart Home Assistant, then add TSUN Local from Settings → Devices & services.

Community research and validation

The 1511 and 02B0 implementations are developed through independent local protocol analysis, real-device observation and hardware validation.

TSUN Local also benefits from public 1097 protocol research by Stefan Allius / s-allius/tsun-gen3-proxy and from real-device compatibility feedback by TheSmartGerman.

Who contributed to what

Credits are intentionally specific: each person is listed only for the work or validation that directly helped TSUN Local.

1097 discovery through real-world testing@TheSmartGerman

Installed TSUN Local on his own hardware. His installation unexpectedly revealed that TSUN Local was also detecting a device using protocol 1097, which led to the discovery and further investigation of this additional protocol family.

1097 protocol & country-profile researchStefan Allius

Public TSUN GEN3 / 1097 protocol research from tsun-gen3-proxy helped inform the experimental 1097 adapter. His country/profile research also provided an important reference for interpreting and validating the inverter country-code/profile data.

02B0 / Solarman protocol cross-referenceDavid Rapan · ha-solarman

Public Home Assistant Solarman protocol and inverter-profile work from ha-solarman provided an independent cross-reference for selected 02B0 register semantics during TSUN Local research. TSUN Local keeps its own hardware-validation policy and does not assign unconfirmed meanings from a reference alone.

Independent hardware validation@dca31

Validated a real Sunology PLAY2 with the normal TSUN Local Home Assistant setup, confirming automatic discovery and the supported local 02B0 path on independent hardware.

Testers and issue reporters

Thanks also to everyone who shares diagnostics, firmware information, protocol captures, device variants, bug reports and test results. When a contribution becomes a concrete compatibility validation, protocol finding or documented project input, it can be added here with the contributor credited for that specific work.

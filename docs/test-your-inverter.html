TSUN Local 1.5.3 · Community hardware validation

Test your TSUN microinverter with TSUN Local

Not listed yet? Your TSUN microinverter may already work locally with Home Assistant even if its exact commercial model has never been tested before.

Install · Discover · Share the result

Your model may already be compatible

TSUN Local 1.5.3 detects supported local protocol families, not only a fixed list of commercial model names. The validated families currently include 1511 and 02B0, while 1097 support remains experimental.

✅ An unlisted model does not mean an unsupported model. If your inverter exposes a supported local protocol, automatic discovery may already be enough.

Three simple steps

1 Install TSUN Local

Add the integration through HACS and restart Home Assistant.

2 Try automatic discovery

Add TSUN Local from Devices & services. No research script or proxy is needed for the first test.

3 Share your result

Send the exact model, firmware, number of PV inputs and the Home Assistant diagnostic when available.

Every result is useful

✅ It works Great — report it so your model can be added to the validated compatibility list.
❌ It does not work Still useful. The result can reveal another logger, protocol revision or hardware variant that needs investigation.

What to share

The community hardware-test form asks for the information needed to understand the result without requiring protocol expertise.

Recommended: exact microinverter model, firmware, number of PV inputs, TSUN Local version, Home Assistant version, test result and Home Assistant diagnostic.

TSUN Local diagnostics omit the device IP address, Monitor SN and AP envelope, but they can still contain production and energy readings. Review attachments before publishing them.
